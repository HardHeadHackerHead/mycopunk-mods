## 0.1.0

- First release: a 3 km track woven through the hub (under its legs, under its belly beneath the north tower, round the tower, over the dome), in the hub's own look, with a light tube whose walls and ceiling you can drive.
- A portal in the hub (walk or drive through), and a launch deck where you arrive: a pad that brings your car, a race pad, the race board, the portal home.
- Power-ups from item boxes (turbo, triple turbo, oil slick, homing zap, shockwave, star shield), fired with Q, with effects everyone sees; boost pads on the road and the tube's ceiling.
- Races for everyone: sign-ups, the grid, start lights, places, a finish order and results. Practice laps with your best kept.
